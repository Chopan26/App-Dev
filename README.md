**My Favorite Movie: Catch Me If You Can**

Genre: Crime, Biography, Drama  
Director: Steven Spielberg  
Starring: Leonardo DiCaprio and Tom Hanks

---

**Why I Like This Movie**

Catch Me If You Can is one of my favorite films because of the chemistry between two great actors, Leonardo DiCaprio and Tom Hanks. The story is based on the real life of Frank Abagnale Jr., who managed to pull off unbelievable cons while he was still a teenager.

---

**What Makes It Interesting**

- The story is fast-paced and easy to follow  
- The characters keep trying to outsmart each other  
- It mixes humor, drama, and suspense in a nice way  
- It shows how clever Frank was even at a young age

---

**Memorable Theme**

The movie talks about identity, family, and the feeling of wanting to belong. The whole film feels like a stylish game of cat and mouse between Frank and the FBI.

A line that stood out to me:  
“Sometimes it’s easier living the lie.” — Frank Abagnale Jr.

---

**Summary**

Overall, it’s a smart and entertaining movie with strong performances from DiCaprio and Hanks. It has a solid story, good pacing, and a balance of emotion and excitement that makes it enjoyable to watch.
